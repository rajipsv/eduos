# EduOS — Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)

## One-line definition

**EduOS is for tuition center owners in India with 40–100+ active students who run enrollment, schedules, fees, and parent updates on WhatsApp and spreadsheets — and want one operations platform, not an LMS.**

---

## Primary ICP (sell here first)

| Attribute | Target |
|-----------|--------|
| **Role** | Owner, director, or head admin (budget holder) |
| **Business** | Academic tuition / coaching center (grades 6–12, board exams, multi-subject) |
| **Size** | 40–100 active students; 3–8 teachers |
| **Locations** | 1 branch to start (multi-branch is upsell) |
| **Geo** | Indian cities (Tier 1–2 coaching clusters) |
| **Tools today** | WhatsApp groups + Excel/Google Sheets; maybe Zoom/Meet for classes |
| **Pain** | Lost leads, manual fee follow-up, schedule chaos, parents not updated on time |
| **Budget** | Can afford **₹18,000/year** platform fee (Starter) |
| **Decision** | Owner decides within **1–2 weeks** of demo |

**Pitch angle:** *“Replace five spreadsheets and a WhatsApp group — CRM, batches, parent comms, and fees in one place.”*

---

## Secondary segments (after primary works)

| Segment | Notes |
|---------|--------|
| **Multi-branch academies** (100+ students) | Starter + branch add-on; needs branch switcher and BI |
| **Hobby / arts / sports centers** | Marketplace fit; ops may differ from academic CRM |
| **Design partners** | 90-day pilot; feedback + case study; converts to Starter |

---

## Anti-personas (deprioritize)

| Segment | Why not now |
|---------|-------------|
| Solo home tutor (under 15 students) | WhatsApp is enough; won’t pay ₹18k/year |
| Pure online course creators | Want LMS/video hosting — not EduOS |
| Schools (K–12 institutions) | Different buyer, procurement, compliance |
| Owners who refuse process change | Won’t adopt CRM or batch discipline |
| “Website only” requests | EduOS is ops platform, not a brochure site |

---

## Qualification checklist (BANT-lite)

Use on first WhatsApp call or from the landing inquiry form:

- [ ] **Owner or director** on the call (not only a teacher)
- [ ] **40+ active students** (or clear path to 40 within 3 months)
- [ ] **Pain is weekly** — fees, leads, schedules, or parent comms break often
- [ ] **Uses WhatsApp** for parents and leads today
- [ ] **Can start pilot within 2 weeks**
- [ ] **Understands** EduOS is **not** an LMS (no course video hosting)

**Strong lead:** 4+ boxes checked. **Weak lead:** under 40 students and “just exploring.”

---

## Discovery questions (landing form + sales call)

1. Center name and city?
2. How many **active students** and **branches**?
3. What type of center? (Academic / coaching / hobby-arts)
4. How do you get **new students** today?
5. What **breaks every month**? (leads, schedule, comms, fees)
6. What do you use **now**? (WhatsApp only, Excel, other app)
7. Which plan interests you? (Starter / multi-branch / design partner pilot)
8. Who **decides** and when can you **start a pilot**?

## Where to find them

- Local coaching clusters and franchise networks
- WhatsApp groups of academy owners
- Guest inquiries on **EduOS marketplace**
- Referrals from design partners
- Search intent: “tuition management software India”, “coaching center CRM”

---

## Product–market fit signals

You have fit when:

- Center admin and teachers **log in weekly**
- They use **CRM + batches + comms**, not one feature only
- Pilot converts to **paid Starter** or refers another center
- Sales cycle **under 2 weeks** from first contact to go-live
