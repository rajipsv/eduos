# Tutorsala — Run your entire tuition center for the fee of one student

Tutorsala replaces the spreadsheets, WhatsApp groups and notebooks you use today with one
simple platform for your center: enquiries, batches, classes, attendance, tests, homework,
fees and parent updates, all in one place.

**It is not an LMS.** You keep teaching the way you do now: in class, on Zoom or on Google Meet.
Tutorsala runs everything around the teaching.

## Features

### 1. Enquiries and admissions (CRM)
- One pipeline for every enquiry: Lead → Enquiry → Demo class → Counselling → Admission → Batch
- Follow-up dates, notes and full activity history for each family
- Enquiries from your public listing and website arrive automatically
- Convert an enquiry into a student in one click; the family login is created for you

### 2. Batches and class schedule
- Pick the days and time, list the topics, and Tutorsala creates every class date
- Each class gets a Google Meet, Zoom or Teams link
- Cancel or reschedule a class, and parents are told on WhatsApp automatically
- Week calendar for the whole center, and a personal schedule for each teacher
- **"What was covered today":** when a class is marked done, the teacher notes what was
  actually taught. Anything unfinished carries over to the next class, and parents can see it.

### 3. Attendance
- Mark present, absent or late for a whole batch in seconds
- Optional WhatsApp alert to parents for absences and late arrivals
- Attendance history and percentage for every student

### 4. Tests, homework and progress
- Record test marks and see averages per student and per batch
- Assign homework; students and parents submit, and teachers grade
- Lesson plans, including AI-suggested lesson plans, quizzes and homework
- Student progress reports, skills tracking and early warnings for students falling behind

### 5. Fees and invoices: monthly **or** per class
- **Monthly fee:** set the fee per batch (with a different fee for a student if needed).
  Invoices are generated each month.
- **Per class attended:** set a fee per class (for example ₹300) and how many classes make a
  bill (for example 8 for twice a week). Only classes marked present are counted. The bill is
  ready the moment the 8th class is attended, with the class dates listed.
- WhatsApp fee reminders with your UPI ID and bank details
- Parents can tap "I've paid" and you confirm with one click
- Collected, expected and overdue amounts at a glance

### 6. Parent and student portal
- Parents get their own login and see only their own child: classes, attendance, marks,
  homework, fees, teacher feedback and what was covered in recent classes
- Message the teacher, set notification preferences, and rate the tutor
- Families with more than one child switch between children; a "student view" shows the
  child's own timetable and homework

### 7. Teacher workspace
- Each teacher sees only their own batches, students, schedule and homework to grade
- Marks attendance, records tests and class notes, plans lessons
- Builds their own public profile (see below)

### 8. Public marketplace listing and tutor profiles
- Your center is listed on Tutorsala's "Find tuitions" page with your tagline, city,
  programs and categories, so parents can filter and find you
- **Tutor profiles:** each tutor adds their own sections, such as certifications, awards,
  results or "why parents choose me", with verification links. Your admin publishes the profile.
- **Parent reviews:** enrolled parents rate and review their child's tutor; you can hide any
  review that shouldn't be public
- Parents send enquiries straight from your listing, and they arrive in your CRM

### 9. WhatsApp and communication
- Automatic messages for absences, test results, homework, class changes and fees
- Templates you can edit, and bulk announcements to a batch
- Works without a WhatsApp Business API account (messages open in WhatsApp Web); connect
  your API account to send automatically

### 10. AI assistant
- Ask questions in plain English: "Which students are at risk?", "Draft a message to
  Aarav's parents about his test"
- Lesson plans, quizzes and parent updates drafted for you
- Uses only the data the person asking is allowed to see

### 11. Multiple branches
- Run several locations under one center account
- Switch between branches; leads, batches and reports can be viewed per branch

## Privacy and security

- **Everyone sees only what they should.** Teachers see their own batches; parents see only
  their own child; your data is never visible to another center.
- **Your keys are protected.** API keys (WhatsApp, OpenAI) are stored encrypted and never
  shown in the browser.
- **Strong sign-in.** Passwords must be strong, and too many wrong attempts are blocked.
- **Phone numbers and emails** of tutors are never shown on the public marketplace.
- **No lost edits.** If two people change the same record at once, nobody's work is silently
  overwritten. The second person sees the latest version.
