# Tutorsala walkthrough — Center admin (owner)

The center admin runs the whole center: enquiries, batches, teachers, students, fees,
communication and reports. Enquiries reach the center through its marketplace listing
(set in Settings → Marketplace listing) or are added by hand in the Lead pipeline. Each section below is one screen: **where** it is, **what it
shows**, **what to click** while recording, and **what to say**.

Before recording, sign in to the center portal with your demo center admin account.
Suggested length: 8–10 minutes for everything, or record each part as its own short video.

---

## 1. Dashboard
**Where:** Overview → Dashboard (opens after sign-in)

**Shows:** today's classes, key numbers (students, batches, leads, fees), AI insights and
quick links.

**Click:** scroll through the cards; click **Full Schedule** and come back; click **Refresh**
on the insights.

**Say:** "This is the owner's morning view: today's classes, new enquiries, pending fees and
students who need attention, all on one screen."

---

## 2. Lead pipeline (CRM)
**Where:** Education CRM → Lead pipeline

**Shows:** every enquiry moving from Lead → Enquiry → Demo class → Counselling → Admission →
Batch allocation.

**Click:**
- **Pipeline:** move a lead to the next stage with **Advance →**.
- **+ New Lead:** add one (name, phone, grade, course, follow-up date).
- **Details:** activity history and notes.
- **WhatsApp:** message the parent.
- **Enroll:** convert the lead into a student and pick the batch.
- **Follow-ups:** leads due for a call today.
- **All Leads:** a searchable list.

**Say:** "From the first call to admission, nothing falls through the cracks. When they join,
Enroll creates the student and the family login in one click."

---

## 3. Batches
**Where:** Academy → Batches

**Shows:** each batch with teacher, schedule, fee, curriculum progress and next class.

**Click:** **+ New Batch**:
1. name, teacher and capacity;
2. **Fee type:** *Monthly fee*, or *Per class attended* (fee per class, plus "bill every … classes", which is automatic: classes per week × 4);
3. pick days and time;
4. paste the topics, one per line;
5. **Auto-Generate Schedule** shows every class date with a meeting link;
6. save.

Also show **Schedule** on a batch card, and **Edit**.

**Say:** "Type your topics once and Tutorsala plans every class, with Google Meet or
Zoom links. Charge monthly, or per class attended. Your choice for each batch."

---

## 4. Class Schedule
**Where:** Academy → Class Schedule

**Shows:** a week calendar of all classes, or a list view.

**Click:**
- switch to the list, with the **Upcoming / All / Completed / Cancelled** tabs;
- **Reschedule** a class (parents are told on WhatsApp);
- **Cancel** with a reason;
- **Mark done**, typing *what was covered* and ticking *not finished — continue next
  class*; the next class then shows "Continue: …";
- **Copy Link**.

**Say:** "Cancel or move a class and parents hear about it instantly. When a class is done,
the teacher notes what was actually covered, and parents can see it."

---

## 5. Teachers
**Where:** Academy → Teachers

**Shows:**
- teacher cards with batches, students and curriculum done;
- profile status: Draft, Waiting for approval or Published;
- the **Parent reviews** list.

**Click:**
- **+ Add Teacher**, **Edit** and **Report**.
- **Public profile:** the tutor's own sections (certifications, awards, results, why
  parents choose me); **Preview**; then **Save & publish**.
- In **Parent reviews**, **Hide** a review.

**Say:** "Each tutor builds their own profile with certificates and achievements. You publish
it, and it appears on your marketplace listing. Parent reviews go live straight away, and you
can hide any that shouldn't be public."

---

## 6. Students
**Where:** Academy → Students

**Shows:** all students with batch, parent contact, attendance and fees.

**Click:** **+ Add Student** (parent phone, fee override, per-class fee override,
billing active or paused); **Edit**.

**Say:** "Every student's details, parent contacts and fee plan in one place."

---

## 7. Fees & Invoices
**Where:** Academy → Fees & Invoices

**Shows:**
- expected, collected and overdue amounts, and parent-reported payments;
- the **Per-class students** table, e.g. "5 of 8 classes · ₹1,500 so far";
- the invoice list for each month.

**Click:**
- **Generate invoices** for the month;
- **Send**, which sends a WhatsApp reminder with your UPI ID;
- **Mark paid**, or **Confirm payment** when a parent has tapped "I've paid";
- **Void** a draft;
- **Send overdue reminders**.

**Say:** "Monthly bills are created in one click. For per-class batches, the bill appears by
itself the moment a student completes 8 classes, with the dates listed. Reminders go
out on WhatsApp with your UPI ID."

---

## 8. Attendance
**Where:** Academy → Attendance

**Shows:** a tap grid per batch and date, plus the attendance trend chart.

**Click:**
1. pick a batch and date;
2. tap **Present / Absent / Late**;
3. tick **Notify parents via WhatsApp**;
4. **Save Attendance**.

For a per-class batch, show the "Bill ready for … 8 classes" message.

**Say:** "Attendance in seconds. Absent and late alerts reach parents automatically, and
per-class fees count only the classes a student actually attended."

---

## 9. Tests & Marks
**Where:** Academy → Tests & Marks

**Shows:** tests per batch with marks and averages.

**Click:** **+ Record Test**, enter the marks, then **Send to Parents**; **Edit** a test.

**Say:** "Enter marks once. Parents get the result on WhatsApp and see it in their portal."

---

## 10. Communication Hub
**Where:** Communication → Communication Hub

**Shows these tabs:**
- **Dashboard**, **Send** and **Templates** (**+ Template**, **Edit**);
- **Automations:** absence alerts, homework, test results, class reminders, with **Enable /
  Disable** and **+ Automation**;
- **Channels**;
- **Reminders:** **Send Class Reminders (24h)** and **Send Homework Due Reminders**;
- **Event Log**.

**Click:** walk through the tabs; **Broadcast to All Parents**.

**Say:** "Every message to parents in one place, with automations so routine updates send
themselves."

---

## 11. Student Success
**Where:** Student Success

**Shows these tabs:**
- **Overview:** success score and students at risk;
- **Journey:** each student's timeline;
- **Assignments:** **+ Assignment** and **Grade**;
- **Interventions:** **Generate & Apply Plan**, **Mark resolved**;
- **Parent Summary:** **Send to Parents**.

Also **Issue Certificate** and **+ Feedback**.

**Say:** "Spot struggling students early, act with a plan, and keep parents in the loop."

---

## 12. Tutor Success
**Where:** Tutor Success

**Shows:**
- a tutor dropdown (admins see every tutor);
- the **Overview** table of all tutors;
- tabs: **Performance**, **Lesson Plans**, **Homework**, **Schedule** (with availability
  slots), **Development** and **Public profile**.

**Click:** switch tutors; **AI Lesson Plan**; **Manage slots**; **Edit & publish** in Public
profile.

**Say:** "See how every tutor is doing: curriculum, homework reviews, student results."

---

## 13. Parent Portal (admin preview)
**Where:** Parent Portal

**Shows:** exactly what a parent sees, for any student (see the family guide).

**Say:** "This is the parent's view. Let me show it properly from a parent's login later."

---

## 14. AI Assistants
**Where:** AI Assistants

**Shows:** a chat with role assistants (Academy Manager, Lesson Assistant, Parent Assistant,
Tutor).

**Click:** ask "Which students are at risk?" or "Draft a message to Aarav's parents about his
test"; **Clear History**.

**Say:** "Ask in plain English. The AI only uses data you are allowed to see."

---

## 15. Business Intelligence
**Where:** Business Intelligence

**Shows these tabs:** **KPIs**, **Trends**, **Lead Analytics**, **Batch Analytics**,
**Predictions**, **Decisions**.

**Say:** "Know where enquiries come from, which batches fill up, and what to do next."

---

## 16. Extensions & Partners
**Where:** Extensions → Extensions & Partners

**Shows:** **Browse / Connected / Connections**, with the Zoom connector, Google Calendar, WhatsApp
Business API, fee templates and partner services.

**Click:** **Details** and **Connect**.

**Say:** "Connect the tools you already use."

---

## 17. Reports
**Where:** System → Reports

**Shows:** **Student Reports** and **Teacher Reports** tables.

**Say:** "Print-ready progress for every student and teacher."

---

## 18. Settings
**Where:** System → Settings

**Shows and click:**
- **Center details:** name and country code.
- **WhatsApp Business API:** the token shows "Saved / Not set" and is never displayed.
- **Billing & Invoices:** invoice prefix, due day, UPI ID, bank details.
- **AI Assistant (OpenAI):** the key is stored encrypted.
- **Marketplace listing:** show or hide, tagline, city, programs, categories, then **Save listing**.
- **Teaching profile:** if the owner also teaches.

**Say:** "Your keys are stored encrypted. Fill in your marketplace listing so parents can
find you by category and city."

---

## Branches (if you have more than one)
The branch switcher at the top filters leads, batches and students by location.
