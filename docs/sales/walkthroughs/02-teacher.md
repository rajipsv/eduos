# Tutorsala walkthrough — Teacher

A teacher sees **only their own batches and students**: their classes, attendance, homework,
lesson plans and their public profile. They don't see fees, enquiries or other teachers.

Before recording, sign in to the teacher portal with a demo teacher account.
Suggested length: 4–5 minutes.

---

## 1. Today
**Where:** My workspace → Today (opens after sign-in)

**Shows:** my batches and students, and upcoming classes with **Join**, **Mark done** and
**Cancel / reschedule**.

**Click:** **Mark done** on a class, type *what was covered*, tick *not finished — continue in
the next class*, then save.

**Say:** "The teacher's day at a glance. After class, one tap records what was actually
covered, and anything unfinished carries over to the next class."

---

## 2. My schedule
**Where:** My schedule

**Shows:** the week calendar of my classes only; the list view with **Upcoming / All / Completed /
Cancelled**; "Covered: …" under finished classes and "Continue: …" on the next one.

**Click:** **Copy Link** or **Join**; **Reschedule** or **Cancel** (parents are told).

**Say:** "Every class with its meeting link. No more searching WhatsApp for the Zoom link."

---

## 3. Mark attendance
**Where:** Mark attendance

**Shows:** my batches only, with Present / Absent / Late for each student.

**Click:** pick a batch and date, tap the statuses, tick **Notify parents via WhatsApp**,
then **Save Attendance**.

**Say:** "Attendance in seconds, and parents of absent students are told automatically."

---

## 4. Homework & plans (my teaching workspace)
**Where:** Homework & plans

**Shows:** **my own** workspace. No other teachers appear; it has "My summary" instead of an
all-tutors table.

**Tabs and clicks:**
- **Overview:** my score, pending homework reviews, upcoming classes.
- **Performance:** student attendance, test average, strengths and recommendations.
- **Lesson Plans:** **AI Lesson Plan** (type a topic) or **+ Lesson Plan**; **Mark
  delivered**, **View**, **Delete**.
- **Homework:** pending submissions, then **Grade**; all my assignments.
- **Schedule:** upcoming classes and **Manage slots** for my availability.
- **Development:** **+ Log PD Activity** (courses, workshops, hours).
- **Public profile:** **Edit my profile**.
  - Write a short introduction.
  - Add your own sections with any heading, e.g. *Certifications*, *Awards*, *Olympiad
    results*, *Why parents choose me*.
  - Add items with details and a verification link.
  - Rename, reorder or delete sections.
  - **Preview**, then **Submit for review**. The center admin publishes it.

**Say:** "Lesson plans in seconds with AI, homework to grade in one list, and my own profile
showing my certificates and results to parents."

---

## 5. Student Success
**Where:** Student Success

**Shows:** my students only, with the success score, journey, assignments and interventions.

**Click:** **+ Assignment**, **Grade**, **+ Feedback**.

**Say:** "I can see which of my students need extra help and act on it."

---

## 6. My students
**Where:** My students

**Shows:** the students in my batches, with parent contact details for WhatsApp.

**Say:** "Only my students. Parent numbers are one tap away."

---

## 7. Messages
**Where:** Messages

**Shows:** messages and templates for my students' parents; **WhatsApp Web**.

**Say:** "Updates to parents without using my personal WhatsApp groups."

---

## What teachers can't see (worth saying in the video)
Fees and invoices, enquiries, other teachers' batches and settings. Tutorsala shows each
person only what they need.
