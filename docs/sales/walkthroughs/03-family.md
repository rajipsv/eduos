# Tutorsala walkthrough — Family (parent and student)

One family login covers the parents and the child. A **Parent / Student** switch at the top
changes the view, and families with more than one child pick the child. Families see **only
their own children**.

Before recording, sign in to the family portal with a demo family account.
Suggested length: 4–5 minutes.

---

## Parent view

### 1. Dashboard (Parent Portal)
**Where:** Dashboard (opens after sign-in)

**Shows:** a child picker ("Viewing as parent of"), then these tabs:

- **Home:** success score, attendance %, pending homework, next classes, and the **Fee due**
  card with **I've paid — notify center**.
- **Progress:** scores, strengths and recommendations, skills, test history, and **Recent
  classes** with what the teacher noted was covered.
- **Homework:** assignments with status; **Mark submitted**.
- **Attendance:** present / absent / late history.
- **Fees:** outstanding and overdue amounts and all invoices.
  - For per-class batches: "5 of 8 classes attended since the last bill · ₹1,500 so far".
  - **I've paid — notify center**, with the payment method and reference.
- **Feedback:** teacher feedback, plus **Rate your child's tutor** (stars and a comment), then
  **Post review**. It appears on the marketplace with the first name only, and you can edit
  it later.
- **Messages:** **Send to Teacher**.
- **Settings:** notification preferences (attendance, tests, homework, feedback),
  preferred channel and language, then **Save Preferences**.

Also **Request Progress Summary** at the top.

**Say:** "Everything about my child in one place: classes, attendance, marks, homework, fees,
and even what was taught in each class. I can pay, then tap 'I've paid', and message the
teacher here."

### 2. Homework
**Where:** Homework

**Shows:** the child's assignments across batches; submit and see grades.

### 3. Messages
**Where:** Messages

**Shows:** messages from the center about my child.

### 4. Find Tuitions (marketplace)
**Where:** Find Tuitions

**Shows:** listed tuition centers with category chips (Academics, Music, Dance, Coding…),
search, city and program filters.

**Click:**
1. pick a category, a city and a program;
2. **View details** on a center;
3. **Our tutors**: open a tutor's profile with certifications, awards, results, verification
   links and parent reviews;
4. **← Back to center**;
5. **Send inquiry**.

**Say:** "Looking for a music or coding class too? Filter by category and city, read the
tutors' profiles and other parents' reviews, and enquire directly."

---

## Student view
Switch the top selector from **Parent** to **Student**.

### 5. Home
**Shows:** the student's own home: today's and upcoming classes, homework due, progress.

### 6. My classes
**Shows:** the timetable with **Join** links, and what was covered in recent classes.

### 7. Homework
**Shows:** assignments; **Submit**, and see grades and feedback.

### 8. Find Tuitions
The same marketplace as above.

**Say:** "Students get their own simple view, just their classes and homework."

## Visitor (not signed in), worth a short clip
Open the website without signing in, scroll to **Find tuitions**, filter, open **View
details** → **Our tutors** → a tutor profile, and send an enquiry. "Parents can find you
and enquire without creating an account."
