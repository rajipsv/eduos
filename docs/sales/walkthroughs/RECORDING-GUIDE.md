# How to record the Tutorsala demo videos

## Pick a recorder (all free on Windows)

| Tool | Best for | How |
|------|----------|-----|
| **Snipping Tool** (built into Windows 11) | Quickest; records one area or window | Press **Win + Shift + R**, drag over the browser window, click **Start**. Click stop when done; then **Save** (MP4). |
| **Clipchamp** (built into Windows 11) | Recording and simple editing (trim, text captions, music) | Open Clipchamp → **Record & create** → **Screen and camera** (or Screen only) → choose the browser tab or window → record. Then trim, add title text, and **Export** at 1080p. |
| **OBS Studio** (free download, obsproject.com) | The most control: webcam corner, microphone levels, long recordings | Add a **Window Capture** of the browser, plus **Audio Input** for the mic. Settings → Output → **MP4**, then **Start Recording**. |

**Recommendation:** use **Clipchamp**. It records and edits in one place, and it can add
captions and a title card.

## Before you record

- **Browser:** use Edge or Chrome in a normal window, maximised. Zoom to **110–125%**
  (Ctrl + plus) so text is readable on phones.
- **Screen resolution:** 1920×1080 is ideal.
- **Close** other tabs, notifications and chat apps. Turn on **Focus** or **Do not disturb**
  (Win + N).
- **Use the demo center, never a real one.** Real names, phone numbers and fees must not
  appear.
- **Never show passwords.** Sign in **before** you start recording, or cut that part out.
- **Rehearse once** using the walkthrough guide, then record.
- **Microphone:** use a headset or earphone mic; record in a quiet room.

## While recording

- **Move the mouse slowly** and pause about 1 second on each thing you point at.
- **Say what problem it solves**, not just what the button is: "No more chasing fees
  on WhatsApp" works better than "This is the fees page".
- **Keep each video short.** Record one role or one feature per clip, 1–3 minutes, and
  join them later.
- **Mistakes are fine.** Keep going and cut them out afterwards.

## Suggested videos

1. **Full demo (8–10 min):** the center admin guide, sections 1, 2, 3, 4, 7, 8, 5 and 18, then a short
   teacher clip (Today → Mark done → Homework & plans), then a family clip (Dashboard tabs
   → Rate tutor → Find Tuitions).
2. **Short highlight (60–90 s), for WhatsApp and ads:**
   - batch → auto schedule;
   - attendance → WhatsApp alert;
   - per-class bill appearing;
   - parent portal;
   - tutor profile on the marketplace.
3. **One clip per role (3–5 min each):** follow 01-center-admin, 02-teacher and 03-family.

## Editing (Clipchamp)

- **Trim** the start and end, and cut any sign-in screen.
- **Add a title card:** "Run your entire tuition center for the fee of one student".
- **Add an end card:**
  - ~~₹36,000~~ **₹18,000/year** (50% off), free 30-day pilot;
  - Book a demo: **www.tutorsala.com/start-page.html**;
  - **hello@tutorsala.com**.
- **Captions:** Clipchamp → **Captions** → auto-generate, then check the spelling of
  "Tutorsala".
- **Export:** **1080p MP4**. For WhatsApp, also export **720p**, which keeps the file
  small.

## Where to share

- **WhatsApp:** the 720p short highlight.
- **Website / YouTube (unlisted):** the full demo, linked from the start page.
- **Sales calls:** play the role clips that matter to that client.
